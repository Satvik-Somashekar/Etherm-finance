# Edge forecasting backtest

Generated by `evaluation/forecast/backtest.ts` from 30 synthetic customers (30,117 bank transactions, 3.9 MB of CSV, from 2023-10), written by fi-core `ForecastBacktestData`. Rolling origin: every month with at least 6 months before it is forecast from those earlier months only (900 customer-months). Lower is better for MASE and wMAPE. MASE below 1 beats repeating last month's figure.

**The synthetic data is regular.** Each customer spends a randomly drawn share of a fixed salary every month, split in fixed proportions, with no trend, no festival months and no life events, and rent, SIPs and premiums never change. Real spending is messier, so these numbers say the code is correct and cheap, not how accurate it will be for real customers.

**Dev set.** Three settings were chosen by looking at these customers (seed 42): how many recent months the models are compared on (6, 9 and 12 tried; 12 kept), how much better a more complex model must be to be picked (0.05, 0.1 and 0.2 tried; 0.1 kept), and leaving smoothing out of what ships. That makes these figures slightly optimistic; FORECAST_HELDOUT.md repeats the run on customers that were not looked at.

## Rung 3: recurring bills

Predicted bill payments: 2160 (2100 monthly, 60 yearly). Paid as predicted (same payee, within 3 days, amount within 1%): 2160 (100.0%). Of 2248 payments to payees the detector recognises as bills when shown all the data, 2160 (96.1%) were predicted for the right month within 3 days. A yearly bill needs two payments before it is recognised, so the first repeat of each insurance premium is missed.

## Cost

One full forecast (shipped point forecasts for every category, the total and the net flow, plus up to twelve re-runs to calibrate the ranges) took 5.2 ms at the median and 11.1 ms at the 95th percentile in Node v22.22.0 on the machine that ran this report, one thread. It reads only the customer's own transactions and keeps a few hundred numbers per series.
